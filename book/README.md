## Contents and layout

The 544-page reconstruction includes preliminary pages, numbered main text,
the folded branching diagram after printed page 168, its blank reverse,
the index through printed page 502, and the 32-page publisher’s catalogue.
PDF page numbers therefore differ from the book’s printed page numbers.

Text pages are typeset text, rather than photographs with an invisible OCR
layer. The diagram and its reverse retain their scanned appearance. Source
paper discoloration and handwritten marks on text pages are omitted.

The layout follows the original printed pagination and line arrangement,
with justified prose, appropriate separate treatment of the title, contents,
index, and catalogue, and odd/even running headers with page-number fields.
Horizontal rules, small caps, italic passages, and superscript references
are preserved where reconstructed. Historical spelling and punctuation spacing
are retained rather than systematically modernized.

## Editing

Install the fonts from [fonts/ttf](../fonts/ttf) before editing. The document
also embeds them, but application support for embedded fonts varies.

The DOCX uses native paragraphs and page sections with many deliberate line
breaks to retain the printed arrangement. It is editable, but is not designed
to reflow freely after large changes. Fonts, layout engines, field updates,
and printer settings can change pagination.

Compact stacked fractions use Word legacy EQ fields with inline vector
previews and PNG compatibility fallbacks. Updating those fields may change
their appearance. The supplied PDF adds searchable fraction values while
preserving the visible outlines. A fresh PDF export from Word or LibreOffice
may handle those values differently.

For the overall method, see [GENERATION.md](../docs/GENERATION.md).
